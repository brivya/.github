# Brivya

**Infrastructure for the Agentic Business Web.**

Brivya helps businesses become **agent-accessible** — discoverable, understandable, callable and transactable by AI agents.

We are building open infrastructure for exposing real business capabilities such as products, services, bookings, orders, quotes, payments and support through protocol-neutral Business Agent interfaces.

## What we are building

- **Brivya Core** — open-source Business Agent runtime
- **Business Agent Manifest** — structured capability definition
- **Protocol adapters** — MCP, A2A, UCP, REST and future protocols
- **SDK & CLI** — developer tooling
- **Reference implementations** — restaurant, hotel, retail, factory and trading-company scenarios
- **Brivya Cloud** — managed Business Agent infrastructure
- **Brivya Network** — future discovery, identity and trust layer

## Mission

> Make every business agent-accessible.

## Principles

- Business-first, not prompt-first
- Protocol-neutral and model-neutral
- Open core
- Typed capabilities and explicit policies
- Human control for high-risk actions
- Auditable transactions
